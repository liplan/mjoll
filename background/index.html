How these articles are made

The articles are based on published studies, reviews and meta-analyses. We cite every source with a reference to the original publication. For some papers, only the abstract was freely available; from those, we only take statements that appear there explicitly. We last checked all sources and their bibliographic details on November 4, 2026, via Crossref and PubMed. Where authors are affiliated with a manufacturer or rate their own studies as of limited reliability, the source list says so.

Research on touch, attention or breathing exercises usually looks at other devices and other situations than Mjoll. There is no study of Mjoll itself so far. Where we transfer findings, we say so.
