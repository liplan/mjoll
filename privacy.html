In short

Mjoll does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app works entirely on your iPhone and does not connect to any server.

Data in the app

The app only stores your settings locally on your device: whether you have seen the introduction and confirmed the safety notes, and the speed you chose for each program. This information never leaves your iPhone. Deleting the app removes it.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple’s privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the page and keep it secure. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing a website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See GitHub’s privacy statement. This website uses no cookies and no tracking. If you choose the light or dark view or a language, only your browser stores that choice. Reading articles aloud uses your device’s own speech output; nothing is sent anywhere.

Contacting us

If you email us, we use your message only to answer you and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object (Art. 15–21 GDPR). You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, for example the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen.
