Home Support Background

Mjoll is a calm iPhone app for haptic meditation. Gentle vibration patterns guide your attention – no sound, nothing to watch. Choose a program, set the time, close your eyes and feel.

Please read the safety notes before your first session. Do not use Mjoll if you have a pacemaker or another implanted medical device.

Where to place it

You feel it best where your iPhone touches bone.

  • Upper chest – rest your iPhone high on your breastbone, just below your throat. Sitting or lying down, screen up or down.
  • Hand – hold it loosely in your open palm.

Ways to use it

  • Meditation – let the pulse be your anchor. When your mind wanders, return to the vibration.
  • Breathwork – choose Wave. Breathe in as it rises, breathe out as it fades.
  • Guided – Herzruhe (German for “heart at rest”) takes ten minutes: a gentle heart rhythm to arrive, slow guided breathing, then a quiet fade.
  • Wind down – before sleep, pick a calm program and a short duration. The session ends on its own.

Set the speed of each program to your own pace – Mjoll remembers it.

Meditation without sound

Mjoll uses touch only. No headphones, no voice, no music. It works with your eyes closed, in a shared room, on the go – and if you are deaf or hard of hearing.

Safe use

Do not use Mjoll if you have a pacemaker, an implanted defibrillator or any other implanted medical device that can be affected by electronic devices or magnets. Mjoll asks you to rest your iPhone on your chest, close to where such devices usually sit. Our clear recommendation: do not use Mjoll at all in this case.

  • Never use Mjoll while driving, cycling or operating machinery. Mjoll asks you to close your eyes and turn your attention inward.
  • Do not fall asleep with your iPhone on your body while it is charging. Long skin contact with a warm device can cause discomfort or burns.
  • Stop if you feel unwell or dizzy. If you have a heart condition or another serious illness, or are pregnant, talk to your doctor before using Mjoll.
  • Mjoll is a wellness app, not a medical device. It does not replace medical or psychological treatment. In a crisis, contact a doctor or emergency services.
