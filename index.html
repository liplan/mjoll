Mjoll is a calm iPhone app for haptic meditation. Rest your iPhone on your chest, hold it in your hand or place it on your forehead while lying down. Gentle vibration patterns guide your attention – no sound, nothing to watch.

Use it for meditation, to accompany breathwork, or to wind down before sleep. Set the speed of each program to your own pace – the app remembers it.

Mjoll is a wellness and relaxation app. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.
